# Brightloop Subscription Manager: Financial Metrics & Reporting Definitions

This document formalizes the definitions, mathematical formulas, and accounting distinctions governing all reporting endpoints and dashboard analytics.

---

## 1. Core Financial Metrics

### 1.1 Active Subscriptions Count
- **Definition:** The total count of subscriptions whose status is currently `'active'`.
- **Database Query:**
  ```sql
  SELECT COUNT(*) FROM subscriptions WHERE status = 'active';
  ```
- **Usage:** Measures active customer subscriber volume currently entitled to service.

---

### 1.2 Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
- **Definition:** The normalized, predictable monthly subscription value generated by all active subscriptions. It measures recurring revenue velocity regardless of billing frequency.
- **Normalization Policy:**
  - `monthly`: `1 * total_amount`
  - `yearly`: `total_amount / 12`
  - `weekly`: `(total_amount * 52) / 12`
  - `daily`: `(total_amount * 365) / 12`
- **Important Distinction:** MRR is a **run-rate metric**, not recognized revenue or collected cash. An annual plan billed at \$2,400 provides \$2,400 in cash upon billing, but contributes exactly \$200/month to MRR.

---

### 1.3 Total Invoiced Amount
- **Definition:** The cumulative face value of all invoices issued to customers within the selected reporting period, excluding cancelled invoices.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Total Invoiced} = \sum (\text{invoices.total\_amount}) \quad \text{for } \text{status} \neq \text{'cancelled'}$$
- **Usage:** Represents gross accounts receivable generated during the selected timeframe.

---

### 1.4 Payments Received (Cash Collections)
- **Definition:** The total cash actually collected and settled within the reporting period through payment gateways (Razorpay) or recorded offline payments.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Payments Received} = \sum (\text{payments.amount}) \quad \text{for } \text{status} = \text{'successful'}$$
- **Usage:** Measures real cash inflow / liquidity for the period.

---

### 1.5 Outstanding Receivables
- **Definition:** The total unpaid balance remaining across all confirmed and partially paid invoices that have not been cancelled.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Outstanding Receivables} = \sum (\text{invoices.amount\_due}) \quad \text{for } \text{status} \in (\text{'confirmed'}, \text{'partially\_paid'})$$
- **Usage:** Represents current unpaid customer claims awaiting reconciliation.

---

### 1.6 Overdue Invoices & Aging Buckets
- **Definition:** Invoices with an outstanding balance (`amount_due > 0`) where `due_date < CURRENT_DATE` and `status != 'cancelled'`.
- **Aging Brackets:**
  1. **1 – 30 Days:** Recently past due; standard first-line collection.
  2. **31 – 60 Days:** Moderately past due; second reminder follow-up.
  3. **61 – 90 Days:** High risk; escalation required.
  4. **90+ Days:** Critical default risk; subject to suspension or bad-debt review.

---

## 2. Fundamental Accounting Distinctions

It is critical to distinguish between the four fundamental revenue and cash concepts:

| Metric Category | Source | Timing | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Invoiced Amount** | `invoices.total_amount` | Date invoice is generated | Billing demand & Accounts Receivable creation |
| **Cash Collections** | `payments.amount` | Date payment settles | Liquidity & bank balance inflow |
| **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)** | `subscriptions.total_amount` | Real-time normalized run-rate | Contractual run-rate velocity & forward growth |
| **Accrual Accounting Revenue** | Earned over service period | Daily / monthly pro-rata | GAAP/IFRS income statement compliance |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Invoiced amount, payments received, MRR, and accounting revenue are **different financial measures** and must not be mixed or substituted for one another.
